Fall Protection Competent Person · Module 8 job aid

Five-Minute Toolbox Talk

One story. One hazard. One commitment. Five minutes that change what people do this afternoon.

UPCHURCH
0:00Story · 1 min

A real story, local and recent. A near miss on this job beats a national statistic. Who, where, what almost happened. No gore.

Example: “Tuesday, Ray stepped outside the cable on Level 3. Somebody had pulled a post at noon. The cable gave two feet and he caught the column.”

Your story
1:00Hazard · 2 min

Show it, on our site. Stand where the hazard is, or point at it. What does it look like here? What’s the fix?

Example: “Every rail post at the hoist landing. If one comes out for a delivery, I know about it and a replacement is up before anyone works there.”

Where on our site / the fix
3:00Commitment · 1 min

One behavior, starting today. Specific enough that you can see whether it happened.

Example: “Starting today, no rail comes down unless I know and a replacement is in place.”

“Starting today…”
4:00Teach-back · 1 min

Ask two people to say it back. Pick the newest person and the most experienced one. Then ask: “What gets in the way of doing this?”

If they can’t say it back, the talk didn’t land. Say it again, shorter.
