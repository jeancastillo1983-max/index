2 · The walk

CheckOKFixN/A
Edges · 6 ft or more to any lower level 1926.501(b)(1)
Perimeter rail continuous: no gaps left from deliveries, pumps or picks
Top rail 39–45 in, midrail about halfway, toeboard ≥ 3½ in where people work below
Cables tensioned and flagged every 6 ft or less; no banding used as rail
Nobody working outside the rail without PFAS to a rated anchor
Holes, skylights and covers · gap 2 in or more 1926.501(b)(4) 1926.502(i)
Every hole covered or railed, including new ones from stripped blockouts
Covers hold 2× the load, are fastened so they can't slide, and say HOLE or COVER
Skylights screened, covered or railed. A dome is not a cover.
No caution tape standing in for a rail or cover
Wall openings and shafts 1926.501(b)(14)
Openings with a sill under 39 in and 6 ft or more outside are railed across
Elevator shaft and stair openings barricaded, fastened, not just leaning in place
Roofs 1926.501(b)(10)–(11) 1926.502(f)
Warning line at least 6 ft from every edge around the work, rope 34–39 in, flagged; moved with the work
Anyone between the line and the edge has PFAS, guardrail, net, or a dedicated monitor
Monitor used alone only on low-slope roofs 50 ft wide or less; monitor has no other duties
Hatches closed or railed; hoist landings gated; parapets under 39 in treated as open edges
Leading edges, forms and rebar 1926.501(b)(2), (b)(5)
Leading-edge crews protected; if a written plan is used, it's on site and its CAZ is marked
Workers on form or rebar faces on PFAS, nets or positioning devices (2 ft max free fall)
PFAS in use 1926.502(d)
Anchors rated 5,000 lb or designed by a qualified person; never rebar, conduit or guardrail
Tie-off at or above D-ring unless gear is rated for foot level; clearance checked
Equipment inspected before use; anything that caught a fall is out of service
Different rule set? Note it and call the right competent person
Scaffolds → Subpart L (10 ft) · Ladders and stairs → Subpart X · Steel erection and decking → Subpart R

Stop work now if you see

An open edge or hole 6 ft or more with people near it · someone outside a rail or past a warning line with no PFAS · tie-off to rebar, conduit or guardrail · a cover that's slid or unmarked over an open hole.
