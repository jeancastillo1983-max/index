Competent vs. qualified: who signs what

Keep it in the truck. When someone asks "is that your call or the engineer's," the answer is on this page.

THE TWO-PART TEST 29 CFR 1926.32(f)

1Capable of identifying existing and predictable hazards.

2Authorized to take prompt corrective measures to eliminate them.

Both, or you're just the person who noticed.

THE QUICK SORT

Does the call need math or a stamp? → Qualified person

Does it need eyes and authority today? → You, the competent person

Is it their own gear before use? → Authorized user, and you check that it happens.

Authorized person

Dee and the crewUses the system. Inspects own gear before each use. Flags and tags problems. Signs: nothing.

Competent person

YouIdentifies hazards, inspects systems, supervises, trains, removes gear from service, stops exposed work. Signs: inspection logs, training records, out-of-service tags.

Qualified person

Engineer / PEDesigns and certifies anchorages and lifelines, writes fall protection plans. Signs: designs, calcs, written plans. 1926.32(m)
