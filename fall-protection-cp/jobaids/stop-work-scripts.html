Stop-Work Scripts

You have the authority to take prompt corrective measures. These are the words that make it work. 1926.32(f)

The five-step script (in this order)

1 StopStop the exposure first. “Hold up. Step back from the edge.”
2 SayWhat you see, specifically. “That cable’s down and the hose is at the edge.”
3 WhyWhy it matters to them: their crew, their schedule, the apprentice watching.
4 FixOffer the fix with a time on it. “Fifteen minutes, two guys, cable back with a gate.”
5 Follow upCome back, check it, say thanks. That’s how next time gets easier.

The schedule push (the superintendent)

They say“Concrete’s on the road. We lose this pour, we lose two days. Put the rail back after.” You say“Marco, if somebody goes over that edge we don’t lose two days, we lose the job for weeks. Give me 15 minutes and two guys: we re-hang the cable with a hose gap gate and the pour goes. I’ll call you when it’s up.” If they push“I’m not stopping your pour. I’m stopping the open edge. Fifteen minutes. Want me to call Jordan to back the plan?” Don’t say“It’s an OSHA violation, I’m not doing it.” (Right rule, no fix, and it stalls the job.)

The pride push (the veteran)

They say“I was tying off before you were born. You’re gonna tell me how to do my job?” You say(Walk over. Keep it low.) “You’ve forgotten more than I know. That cable is built for somebody leaning on it, not a falling body. Dee copies everything you do. Help me show her where the beam clamp goes?” If they push“I’m not writing anybody up. I need you clipped overhead before you go back out. Then I need you teaching it.” Don’t sayAnything in front of the crew that makes them lose face. You’ll get compliance while you watch, and nothing after.

“Not your guys, not your problem” (another employer’s crew)

They say“Not your guys, not your problem. We’ve got our own safety program.” You say“I hear you. He’s exposed right now, on this deck, where my crew walks. Have him step back from the opening. Let’s get your foreman and the super and fix it. I can loan you two SRLs and a beam clamp until yours show up.” If they push“Then we’ll stop that task until the super is here. Everything else keeps going.” (Know what your contract says about site-wide stop-work authority.) Don’t say“Pack it up, you’re off my site.” (Stops it, but turns a fix into a fight.)

Words that work

Instead ofSay
“Be careful out there.”“Hold up. Come inside the rail.”
“That’s a violation.”“That edge is open. Here’s how we close it in 15 minutes.”
“Safety first.”“If Dee goes over, this job stops for weeks.”
“You should know better.”“You’re the one the crew watches. Help me show them.”
