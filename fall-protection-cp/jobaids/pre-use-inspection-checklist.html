Head to toe, before it goes on. Bend webbing into a U to open hidden cuts. If anything fails: tag it, pull it, tell your competent person. 1926.502(d)(21)

Anything fail? Do this, today

  1. Off the worker and out of the gang box.
  2. Tag: DO NOT USE · date · defect · your name.
  3. Lock it up or red bin, away from good gear. Log it.
  4. Replacement to the worker the same shift.
  5. Destroy (cut webbing) or return to the maker, per the manual.

It caught a fall?

Out of service immediately. It stays out until a competent person determines it is undamaged and fit for reuse. Most manufacturers say destroy it. SRLs go back to the maker. 1926.502(d)(19)

Pre-use = the worker, every use. Periodic = a competent person, at least yearly, written (page 2). ANSI/ASSP Z359.2.FPCP-2026 · M6 · p1

Every harness, lanyard, SRL, connector and portable anchor, at intervals of no more than one year; more often per the maker or harsh site conditions. ANSI/ASSP Z359.2
