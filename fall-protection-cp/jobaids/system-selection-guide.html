The numbers

Check these with a tape measure, not a feeling.

UPCHURCH
SystemWhat you checkReceipt
GuardrailTop rail 42 in ± 3 in (39–45). Holds 200 lb out or down within 2 in of the top, never deflects below 39 in. Midrail about halfway, holds 150 lb. Rails ≥ ¼ in thick. No steel or plastic banding as rails. Wire-rope top rail flagged every 6 ft or less.1926.502(b)
ToeboardAt least 3½ in tall, ¼ in max gap at the bottom, holds 50 lb. Required where people or material could be hit from below.1926.502(j)
CoverHolds 2× the weight of workers, equipment and materials on it (roadways: 2× max axle load). Secured so it can't move. Marked HOLE or COVER, or color-coded.1926.502(i)
Safety netAs close under the work as practical, never more than 30 ft below. Drop-tested or certified. Inspected at least weekly and after anything that could damage it.1926.502(c)
Warning lineAt least 6 ft from every roof edge around the work area (10 ft perpendicular to mechanical equipment travel). Rope 34–39 in high, flagged every 6 ft or less, stanchions that won't tip at 16 lb.1926.502(f)
Safety monitorA competent person, on the same surface, within sight, close enough to talk, with no other duties. No mechanical equipment where a monitor is the only protection on a low-slope roof.1926.502(h)
PositioningFree fall 2 ft max. Anchor holds 2× the impact load or 3,000 lb, whichever is greater.1926.502(e)
PFASAnchor 5,000 lb per person, or designed by a qualified person with a safety factor of 2. Free fall 6 ft max, no contact with a lower level, stop within 3½ ft, 1,800 lb max arresting force. Never anchored to a guardrail.1926.502(d)

Safety monitor alone? Almost never.

Only for roofing work on a low-slope roof 50 ft wide or less, or inside a written fall protection plan. Never on a deck edge, a landing zone, or a roof wider than 50 ft by itself. 1926.501(b)(10)

Written fall protection plan

Only for leading edge, precast and residential work, and only when you can document that conventional protection is infeasible or a greater hazard. Written by a qualified person for this site, kept on site, implemented under a competent person, with each location set up as a controlled access zone. 1926.502(k)

When you say no, bring a yes. Pulling a rail? Offer the gated span, the SRL to the beam above, the lift from below. Then you're the person who solved the problem, not the one who stopped the job.
