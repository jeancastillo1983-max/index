1 Walks an active work area and identifies existing and predictable fall hazards Look for: names every exposure at 6 ft or more (edges, holes, skylights, wall openings, leading edges); spots at least one hazard that is coming (tomorrow's openings, a rail that will be pulled); checks covers are marked and secured; corrects or escalates on the spot. 1926.32(f) 1926.501(b)
2 Selects a compliant system for two real tasks and explains why Look for: works down the hierarchy (eliminate, guard, restrain, arrest, administrative); picks a system that fits the task and the surface; knows monitoring alone is only for low-slope roofing on roofs 50 ft wide or less; does not offer a fall protection plan as a shortcut. 1926.502 A10.32
3 Inspects a harness, a lanyard or SRL, and an anchor, and correctly tags one item Look for: checks webbing, stitching, hardware, labels and impact indicators in order; tests SRL lock-up and retraction; confirms the anchor is rated 5,000 lb or qualified-person designed; tags a defective or impact-loaded item out of service and removes it. 1926.502(d)(19) (d)(21) Z359.2
4 Calculates fall clearance for an actual tie-off point Look for: measures the real anchor height and the drop to the next level; adds lanyard + 3.5 ft deceleration + 1 ft stretch + D-ring height − anchor height + 2 ft margin (or uses the manufacturer's chart for an SRL); confirms free fall stays at or under 6 ft; changes the anchor or connector if it does not clear. 1926.502(d)(16)
5 Inspects a guardrail run and a hole cover against spec Look for: measures top rail at 42 in ± 3 in, checks midrail, gaps and cable sag, pushes the rail to judge the 200 lb requirement; confirms the cover holds 2× the load, is secured, and is marked HOLE or COVER. 1926.502(b) 1926.502(i)
6 Walks through the site rescue plan and confirms equipment is on hand Look for: states how a suspended worker is reached at this location within minutes; shows the staged rescue gear and its inspection status; names the trained rescuers on shift; treats 911 as a parallel call, not the plan. 1926.502(d)(20) Z359.4
7 Demonstrates a stop-work conversation (role-play with the safety manager is acceptable) Look for: stops the exposure first; stays calm and respectful under schedule or pride pushback; offers the fastest compliant fix; informs the superintendent and does not back down on the exposure. 1926.32(f)
8 Reviews a worker training certification record for completeness Look for: confirms the record has the employee's name or identity, the training date(s), and the trainer's or employer's signature; confirms a competent person trained; names when retraining is required (workplace change, equipment change, inadequate knowledge or use). 1926.503(b) (c)
