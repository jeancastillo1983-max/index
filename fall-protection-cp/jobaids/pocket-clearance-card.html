Card 1 · The stack

Clearance you need below the deck

RC = L + DD + HS + H − A + SM
L lanyard 6 ftDD decel 3.5 ftHS stretch 1 ft
H D-ring to boots 5 ftSM margin 2 ft
A = anchor height above your boots (feet 0 · D-ring 5 · overhead 7)
Free fall = L + H − A. Must be ≤ 6 ft. 1926.502(d)(16)
Card 2 · Worked example

Dee, Level 3 deck edge

6 ft lanyard · anchor at her boots (A = 0) · 14 ft to the slab
6 + 3.5 + 1 + 5 − 0 + 2 = 17.5 ft
Free fall 11 ft (over 6) · needs 17.5, has 14 → SHE HITS
Fix 1: beam clamp 7 ft up → 10.5 ft CLEAR
Fix 2: overhead SRL → 8.5 ft CLEAR

Card 4 · SRLs

Self-retracting lifelines

Overhead SRL: 2 + 3.5 + 1 + 2 = 8.5 ft
  • Mount overhead, close to plumb over the work.
  • Anchor at your feet near an edge? SRL-LE only, with the maker's setback and clearance chart.
  • Pre-use: line retracts fully; a hard pull locks it; impact indicator intact.
ANSI/ASSP Z359.14
Card 5 · Swing fall

Off to the side = extra drop + a swing

Extra drop = R − √(R² − d²)
R = line length · d = how far you are off to the side
R = 12 ft lined = 6 ftd = 8 ftd = 10 ft
Extra drop1.6 ft3.1 ft5.4 ft
Fix: move the anchor over the work. Clear columns and stock from the swing path.
Card 6 · Anchor or not

5,000 lb per worker, or engineered

Either it holds 5,000 lb per worker attached, or a qualified person designed it with a safety factor of 2. 1926.502(d)(15)
NEVER: guardrail (d)(23), conduit, sprinkler pipe, ductwork, rebar dowels, ladders, hoists.
Ask a qualified person: bar joists, horizontal lifelines (d)(8), anything without a tag or a design.
Card 7 · Connectors

Hooks and carabiners

  • Locking (self-closing, self-locking) only. 1926.502(d)(5)
  • Compatible with the D-ring or anchor: no roll-out.
  • Never to webbing, to each other, to a loaded D-ring, or to a horizontal lifeline, unless designed for it. (d)(6)
  • No knots to shorten a lanyard. Get the right one.
  • Body belts are not fall arrest.
Card 8 · Before anyone clips in

Three questions

1. What is it rated? 5,000 lb or engineered.
2. Where is it? Overhead beats D-ring. Feet = special gear.
3. How far down is the next thing? Do the stack.
System limits: free fall ≤ 6 ft · decel ≤ 3.5 ft · ≤ 1,800 lb on the body · no contact with the level below.
Values per course standard: DD 3.5 ft, HS 1 ft, H 5 ft, SM 2 ft. Use the manufacturer's clearance chart when it says more. FPCP-2026 · M5
