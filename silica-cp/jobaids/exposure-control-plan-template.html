Housekeeping

Two HEPA vacs on each active level. Wet sweep or HEPA vac only. Brooms and air hoses are not used for silica dust; the clothes booth at the trailer is the only compressed air for clothing.

Restricting access

Barrier tape and "Silica work area" signs at every entry to an active cut. Indoor cutting and cleanup scheduled 7–9 a.m. before drywall and MEP crews start, or the area is closed. The CP tells every other foreman on the floor before dusty work starts. Breaks and lunch in the trailer, never in the work area.

Competent person

Tasha R., foreman, cell on the trailer board. Inspects each active silica task at the start of the shift, after lunch, and whenever a task, crew, location or weather changes. Daily checklist filed in the trailer.

Review and location

Reviewed 01/15/2026 by the safety manager; next review by 01/15/2027 or sooner if tasks change. Copy in the job trailer and in the site app. Available to workers, their representatives, OSHA and NIOSH on request.
