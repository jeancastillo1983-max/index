Crew talk and stop-work scripts

Short, specific, and with the fix in the same breath. Practice these out loud before you need them.

SituationSayIf they push back
Dry cut, no control"Hold the cut. That saw needs water on the blade. Tank is by the gang box; we are back on in five.""Five minutes now or a cloud on the whole floor. I will help you fill it."
Control not working"Your vac is pulling half what it should. Swap the filter before the next joint.""A shroud with no suction is just a hat. It is not the control."
Wrong respirator"Indoors, that jackhammer needs your fit-tested half mask from the first minute, not after lunch.""The 4-hour rule is for outside. In here it is APF 10 the whole time."
Broom or air hose"Not with the broom or the air. HEPA vac and a wet sweep. I will grab the second vac.""Same fifteen minutes. And the plumbers do not breathe it."
Another employer's crew"We are cutting here for the next hour. Can your guys start on the east side and come back at 10? I will tell you when we are clean.""It is our dust, so it is our job to keep you out of it."
Super pushing the schedule"I hear you. Here is how we keep the schedule: second saw with water, and we cut while the other crew is at lunch.""If OSHA or the owner walks in on a dry cut, we lose more than an hour."
"It's just dust""Walt said that too. The dust that hurts you is the part you cannot see. The water is for the part you cannot see.""I am not asking you to like it. I am asking you to still be breathing at 70."

1 · Stop the exposure

Hold the task or move the people. Do it first.

2 · Name the fix

Give the specific control and how long it will take.

3 · Tell and write it

Tell the supervisor, note it on the daily checklist.
